Questions to ask your surgeon

Take this with you. You are allowed to ask all of it, and a good surgeon will be glad you did.

About them

  • Are you certified by the American Board of Plastic Surgery?
  • Was your training specifically in plastic surgery, and for how many years?
  • Do you have hospital privileges to do this procedure, and where?
  • If it's an office facility, is it accredited, state-licensed or Medicare-certified?

About you

  • Am I a good candidate for this?
  • Which technique do you recommend for me, and why that one?
  • What will be expected of me to get the best result?
  • Where and how will the procedure be done?

About what follows

  • How long is recovery, and what help will I need at home?
  • What are the risks and complications, and how do you handle them if they happen?
  • What are my options if I'm unhappy with the result?
  • Can I see before-and-after photos, and what result is realistic for me?

Two more worth adding, from the pages in this site: will I need more than one operation, and how many of this particular procedure do you do each year?

Adapted from the American Society of Plastic Surgeons consultation checklist.
